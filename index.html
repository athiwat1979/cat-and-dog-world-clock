How to Use Cat and Dog World Clock

Simple 3 steps: 1. Choose a city→ 2. Choose a cat or dog style→ 3. Set an alarm

More instructions / FAQ

Welcome to Cat and Dog World Clock—a simple, customizable analog world clock designed to stay open on your screen all day.

  • Nearly 150 Cities in 3 Steps: ① Click + Add Clock → ② Search for and select a city → ③ Click Add Clock in the dialog.
  • Make It Minimal Mode: Click the Make It Minimal button to shrink and simplify the layout for a distraction-free workspace. If your browser or screen is in Full Screen mode, exit Full Screen first.
  • Automatic Background Colors: The clock card background changes automatically according to each city's local time: at 06:00, 12:00, 18:00, and 00:00.
  • Cat & Dog Animations: Cute cat and dog animations appear at scheduled times. To turn them off, click the Animation button on that clock.
  • Multiple Alarms: Click Alarm Set on a clock, then click Add alarm to add multiple alarms. Set each time and switch it On.
  • Clock Face Themes: Click a clock face and choose a style to change its appearance.
  • Remove a Clock: Hover over the upper-right corner of a clock card to reveal Delete, then click it.
  • Stop a Ringing Alarm: When an alarm rings, click Stop alarm in the on-screen dialog to silence it.
  • Custom City Clocks: Click Clock Customize, enter any city name, and set its GMT offset manually (−12:00 to +14:00).
  • Auto-Save Settings: Your preferred cities, window size, and color settings are saved automatically for continuous daily use.
