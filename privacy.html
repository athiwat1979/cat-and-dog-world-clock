Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This notice describes how Cat and Dog World Clock currently handles information. The website does not ask you to create an account.

Information stored in your browser

The app uses your browser's local storage on this device to remember your clock list and preferences, custom clock names and UTC offsets, alarm times and settings, and a page-view counter. The counter is stored only in this browser; it is not a site-wide visitor count. The app also reads your device's time-zone setting to choose an initial clock.

This information is stored locally by your browser and is not sent to the site owner by the app. It remains until you clear this site's browser data. Clearing it may remove saved clocks, alarms, and the local counter. Your browser may keep separate storage for different browsers or devices.

Cookies, advertising, and analytics

The current version does not set cookies and does not include advertising or analytics services. If advertising or analytics are added later, this policy should be updated to identify those services and explain their data practices before they are enabled. Third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy policies.

Website hosting

If you visit this site online, the hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as an IP address, browser details, and the time and files requested, to deliver and protect the website. This is handled under the hosting provider's own terms and privacy policy; the app itself does not receive these server logs.

Your choices

You can remove locally saved information by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. If browser storage is unavailable or disabled, the app may not be able to remember your clocks and preferences.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website or its services change. The latest version will be posted on this page. No contact email is published on this site at this time.

This page is a plain-language description of the current website, not legal advice. Before enabling monetization or third-party services, review the applicable provider requirements and laws for your audience.
