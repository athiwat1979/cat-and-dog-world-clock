Cat and Dog World Clock

About

Cat and Dog World Clock is a playful way to check the time in cities around the world. It brings practical time-zone tools together with friendly cat and dog animations.

What you can do

  • Add clocks for cities or create a custom clock with a UTC offset.
  • Choose cat or dog animations and set local alarms for a clock.
  • See a starting clock based on your device's time zone.

Your clock choices and settings are saved in your browser on this device. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
